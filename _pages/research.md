My research sits at the intersection of **autonomous driving**, **world models**, and **simulation-based validation**. I am interested in how learned models of traffic and driving dynamics can support **planning-oriented end-to-end autonomy** and **closed-loop safety testing**, rather than only improving open-loop prediction metrics.

Starting in **Fall 2027**, I will pursue direct Ph.D. studies in **Autonomous Driving and Autonomous Transportation** at the **School of Transportation Engineering**, **Tongji University**.

## Major Research Areas

1. **World Models for Driving** — learning predictive representations of traffic evolution and action-conditioned futures  
2. **Planning-Oriented End-to-End Autonomy** — connecting perception, prediction, and planning under a unified decision objective  
3. **Closed-Loop Simulation & Safety Testing** — co-simulation, reactive evaluation, and risk-aware validation for autonomous systems

---

### 1 World Models for Driving

World models aim to answer a planning-relevant question: *if the ego vehicle takes an action, how will the traffic scene evolve?* My work focuses on **traffic/driving world models** that support downstream decision-making and testing, including risk perception, future-scene imagination, and coupling with simulators.

**Ongoing & recent work**

- **Undergraduate thesis** (*in progress*): *Joint Simulation Methods for Traffic World Models Oriented Toward Autonomous Driving Testing* — building a **co-simulation** framework that links traffic world models with simulation environments for AD testing.
- **Risk Perception Technology Based on World Models** (*Project Leader*, 2025–2026): generative simulation setup, world-model design/training, and risk inference for latent hazard identification in autonomous driving.

---

### 2 Planning-Oriented End-to-End Autonomy

End-to-end driving is moving from pure trajectory imitation toward **action selection supported by verifiable evidence**. I study how world models, reinforcement learning, and multimodal foundation models can improve **planning-relevant representations** and **candidate trajectory ranking** in closed-loop or proxy closed-loop settings.

### 3 Closed-Loop Simulation & Safety Testing

Reliable deployment requires evaluation beyond open-loop ADE/FDE. I work on **traffic co-simulation**, **reactive closed-loop testing**, and tooling for building and diagnosing simulation cases (e.g., signalized intersections, pedestrian crossings, roundabouts) in professional traffic simulators.
