I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Transportation Engineering** at the **School of Transportation Engineering**, **[Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/)**. My research is centered on **autonomous driving** as the main through-line, connecting **decision-making**, **learning-based planning and control**, and **closed-loop simulation**.

I build autonomous driving methods around **world models**, **reinforcement learning**, **game-theoretic interaction modeling**, and **simulation-driven validation**, with a focus on improving **safety**, **robustness**, and **reliability** in complex traffic environments.

# 🔬 Research Experience

### Risk Perception Technology Based on World Models
*Project Leader · 2025 – 2026 · Autonomous Driving*

- **Task:** Build a risk-aware autonomous driving system that can predict, identify, and evaluate latent safety hazards in complex driving scenarios.
- **Models / Algorithms:** Generative simulation environments, world-model-based risk inference, end-to-end learning from world model training to risk-aware decision assessment, with emphasis on out-of-distribution generalization.
- **My Contribution:** Led project execution across the full pipeline, including simulation environment setup, world model architecture design and training, and risk reasoning module development.
- **Results:** **National First Prize**, National University Intelligent Transportation Competition (*2026.07*); **Shanghai First Prize**, 5th Shanghai College Student Transportation Science and Technology Competition (*2025.11*).
- **Toolchain:** Python, deep learning, generative simulation, closed-loop autonomous driving experimentation.

### Adversarial Game-Theoretic Interactive Model for Autonomous Lane Changing
*Project Leader · 2023 – 2024 · Autonomous Driving Decision-Making*

- **Task:** Improve autonomous lane-changing decisions under strong interaction with pedestrians and surrounding traffic in campus intelligent transport scenarios.
- **Models / Algorithms:** Non-cooperative game-theoretic decision framework, strategy-space design, car-following and lane-changing model refinement, utility-based interaction modeling.
- **My Contribution:** Designed and implemented the game-theoretic interaction model, defined the strategy space, revised lane-changing logic, and conducted MATLAB-based simulation and calibration.
- **Results:** **Third Prize (Sprouting Group)**, 10th Tongji University Innovation & Entrepreneurship Academic Forum.
- **Toolchain:** MATLAB, traffic simulation, game-theoretic modeling.

### Flight Prediction and Gate Assignment Optimization with Transformer and Deep RL
*Core Member · 2024 – 2025 · Learning-Based Prediction and Control*

- **Task:** Support dynamic flight prediction and intelligent gate assignment for airport operations.
- **Models / Algorithms:** Transformer-based flight delay forecasting, policy-gradient reinforcement learning for gate assignment optimization, prediction-control system integration.
- **My Contribution:** Led core model training, including data preprocessing, Transformer fine-tuning, and iterative RL policy network training.
- **Results:** **Silver Award**, China International College Student Innovation Competition (2025) – University-level Selection.
- **Toolchain:** Python, Transformer, deep reinforcement learning, model deployment and system integration.
