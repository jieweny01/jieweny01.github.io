I am a final-year undergraduate student majoring in **Transportation Engineering** at the **School of Transportation Engineering**, **[Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/)**, and I will begin direct **Ph.D.** studies in **Autonomous Driving and Autonomous Transportation** at **Tongji University** in **Fall 2027**. My research interests lie in **autonomous driving** and **artificial intelligence**, with a focus on **world models for driving**, **planning-oriented end-to-end autonomy**, and **closed-loop simulation-based testing**.

My work emphasizes **end-to-end learning**, **reinforcement learning for planning and control**, and **traffic co-simulation** for safety-critical validation. I am passionate about building methods that improve **safety**, **robustness**, and **reliability** in real-world autonomous systems.

# Research

My research sits at the intersection of **autonomous driving**, **world models**, and **simulation-based validation**. Major areas:

1. **World Models for Driving** — predictive representations of traffic evolution and action-conditioned futures  
2. **Planning-Oriented End-to-End Autonomy** — connecting perception, prediction, and planning under a unified decision objective  
3. **Closed-Loop Simulation & Safety Testing** — co-simulation, reactive evaluation, and risk-aware validation

### Ongoing Work

- **Undergraduate thesis** (*in progress*): *Joint Simulation Methods for Traffic World Models Oriented Toward Autonomous Driving Testing* — a **co-simulation** framework linking traffic world models with simulation environments for autonomous driving testing.
